Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

This policy explains what information Starstruction collects through the Starstruct platform, how it is used, and the choices available to the people who use it. It applies to starstruction.com, to the Starstruct application at starstruct.ai, and to any related services we operate.

Who we are

Starstruction provides work management software to construction and roofing contractors. Our customers are businesses. The individuals who use the software are those businesses' employees and crew members.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information — name, work email address, mobile number, job role, and the company you belong to.
  • Business content — the records you create in the software: leads, customers, job addresses, photographs, measurements, estimates, proposals, invoices, schedules, and messages between colleagues.
  • Enquiries — anything you send us through a demo request or a support conversation.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device data — pages viewed, actions taken, browser and device type, and IP address, used to operate and improve the service.
  • Cookies — used to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences. We do not use advertising cookies.

Information from connected services

If you connect a third-party account such as Google Workspace, we access only what is needed for the feature you enabled — for example sending email on your behalf, or writing your assigned jobs to your calendar. We do not read, store, or use that data for any other purpose, and you can disconnect at any time from the integrations screen.

How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and secure the platform.
  • To send operational notifications about your work — see Text message notifications below.
  • To support you, and to respond to your enquiries.
  • To improve features, diagnose problems, and prevent abuse.
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your business content to advertise to you or to anyone else.

Text message (SMS) notifications

Starstruction sends operational text message notifications to people who use the Starstruct application and who have enrolled themselves.

Who is enrolled. Only users with a Starstruct account who have individually switched on SMS notifications in their own account settings. There is no public sign-up. We do not purchase, rent, or import mobile numbers from any third party.

How consent is given. SMS notifications are off by default for every user and every notification type. A user enters their own mobile number on their own profile and switches on each notification type they wish to receive. Switching a type on is the act of consent. No number is entered on a user's behalf, and an administrator cannot enable SMS for another user. The full flow, with screenshots, is documented at starstruction.com/sms-notifications.

What we send. Operational alerts about work inside the application: a new job lead entered into your workspace, a new message posted by a colleague on a job, and photos uploaded to a job. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages.

Message frequency. Frequency varies with your own activity in the application and with which notification types you have switched on. There is no scheduled or bulk send.

Cost. Message and data rates may apply under your own mobile plan. Starstruction does not charge you for these messages.

How to stop. Reply STOP to any message to stop all further texts immediately, or switch the SMS toggles off in Settings › Notifications at any time. Reply START to resume. Reply HELP for help.

Sharing. We do not sell, rent, or share user mobile numbers or SMS consent with any third party. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile numbers are transmitted only to our messaging carrier for the sole purpose of delivering the notifications described above, and are removed when an account is closed.

How we share information

We share information only in these circumstances:

  • Within your company's workspace — colleagues in the same account can see the records and messages you create there.
  • Service providers — hosting, database, email, and messaging providers who process data on our instructions and only to deliver the service. Our messaging carrier receives mobile numbers solely to deliver the notifications described above.
  • Legal — where required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.
  • Business transfer — if the business is acquired or merged, subject to this policy.

We do not sell personal information to anyone, for any purpose.

Retention

We keep account and business content for as long as the account is active, and for a limited period afterwards to meet legal and accounting obligations. Mobile numbers are removed when an account is closed. A customer may request deletion of their workspace data at any time.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is restricted to the account it belongs to, and administrative access is limited to staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected customers of a breach as the law requires.

Your choices

  • Turn any notification, by channel, on or off in Settings › Notifications.
  • Reply STOP to any text message to stop all texts.
  • Disconnect a linked Google account from the integrations screen.
  • Ask us for a copy of your personal information, or ask us to correct or delete it, using the contact details below. Deletion of business records may require your employer's authorisation, since the account belongs to them.

Children

Starstruct is a workplace tool and is not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We will post any change on this page and update the date at the top. Material changes affecting how we use personal information will be notified in the application.
