Our Story

Built on a rooftop.
Designed for the future.

We built the platform we always wished existed — powered by AI, built for the field.

Construction intelligence, finally.

Starstruction was born from a simple frustration: the best contractors in the world were still running their businesses on spreadsheets, whiteboards, and gut instinct. Not because they didn't want better tools — but because nothing built for construction actually worked the way construction works.

So we built it ourselves. Starting with roofing — one of the most complex, margin-sensitive trades in construction — and designing a platform that handles the entire lifecycle of a job, from the first satellite measurement to the final payment.

Today Starstruction serves roofing contractors and general contractors who refuse to let outdated software slow them down.
